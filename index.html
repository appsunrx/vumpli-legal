Vumpli Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you play Vumpli, the block puzzle game on Google Play (com.vumpli.blocks) and the App Store. "We" and "us" mean the developer of Vumpli, as named on the game's store page.

The short version

  • There's no account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device. We don't run any server that receives them.
  • We, the developer, don't collect personal data about you. The game has no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. When your device is online, Google's ad software collects some device and usage information to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. This is described below.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you're asked for consent first, and you can change your choice anytime in Settings → Privacy settings.
  • Quest reminders are local notifications, and only if you turn them on.

What stays on your device

Vumpli saves these on your phone or tablet so you can pick up where you left off:

  • Your best score, the game in progress (board, pieces and score) and your recent Daily Pattern results
  • Your Vumpli World progress: levels won, Stars, restored islands, your style choices and decorations
  • Daily quest progress and your streak
  • Your settings: sound, vibration, reduce motion, hints, language, quest reminders, and whether you've finished the tutorial

The game doesn't send any of this to us or to anyone else. We can't see it, so we also can't restore it if it's lost. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

If your device's own backup is on (for example Android backup to your Google account, or iCloud Backup), your phone may include the game's files in that backup. The backup is kept by Google or Apple in your own account. We have no access to it.

Ads: Google AdMob

Vumpli shows ads from Google AdMob, an advertising service of Google. It is the only third party that receives data from the game, and it processes that data under Google's own policies.

Today the only ads are optional videos: in Classic, when you're out of rotations, you can choose to watch one to get an extra rotation. There are no ads in the Daily Pattern or in Vumpli World. So that a video is ready when you want one, the game loads ads in the background while your device is online. This means Google may receive the information below even if you never watch an ad.

When ads are requested or shown, Google's ad software (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) collects:

  • Device identifiers. On Android, the advertising ID and the app set ID. On iPhone and iPad, an identifier tied to the app or developer. Vumpli never asks for permission to track you on iOS, so it can't read your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and doesn't track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location.
  • Ad interactions, such as app launches, taps, video views and which ads were shown.
  • Diagnostics, such as performance information about the app and the ad software (for example launch time, hang rate or energy use) and crash information.

Google uses this information to show ads, to measure and report on them, and to prevent fraud and abuse. Whether ads are personalized depends on the choices you make in the consent message (where it's shown) and on your device settings. The information is encrypted in transit, and Google may process it on servers outside your country.

We don't receive this information in a form that identifies you. AdMob gives us only summary reports, such as how many ads were shown.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and other regions where it's required). When the game starts, it checks with Google's consent service, the User Messaging Platform, whether a consent message applies where you are. That check sends basic technical information, such as your IP address, to Google. Where a message applies, it's shown before any ads are requested, and Google uses your data for personalized ads only if you agree.
  • Change or withdraw consent. In the game, open Settings → Privacy settings. This option appears wherever a consent message applies.
  • Android advertising ID. Open your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some devices Settings → Google → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID. After you delete it, apps can no longer use it for ads.
  • iPhone and iPad. Vumpli doesn't ask to track you, so there's nothing to turn off for this game. You can review tracking for all apps in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Play offline. Vumpli works without an internet connection. Offline, no ads are requested and nothing is sent to Google.

Notifications

Quest reminders are optional. The game asks you first, and only after you say yes does your device ask for notification permission (on devices that require it). Reminders are scheduled on your device: at most two a day, only for quests you haven't finished yet. No push service is used, and nothing about them is sent to a server. You can turn them off anytime in Settings → Quest reminders or in your device's notification settings.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads. The game itself works offline.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on quest reminders.
  • Advertising ID (Android): added by Google's ad software so it can read the advertising ID for ads.
  • Google's ad software also declares a few technical permissions on Android, for example for Android's Privacy Sandbox ad features and for background tasks. None of them show a prompt.

Vumpli doesn't ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Analytics and crash reports

Vumpli doesn't include any analytics, crash-reporting or remote-configuration service, so nothing about how you play is sent to us. If you've allowed your device to share diagnostics with app developers, Google Play or Apple may give us crash and performance reports for the game. Those reports don't identify you.

Children

Vumpli is not directed to children under 13, and its store target audience is 13 and older, because the game shows ads from Google. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you're a parent or guardian and have a question, contact us at info@usoft.uz.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under the GDPR in the EEA, the UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

We don't hold personal data about you, so there's usually nothing for us to look up or delete. The ad data described above is held by Google, and Google's Privacy Policy explains how to exercise your rights with Google. You're welcome to contact us with any request or question, and we'll help where we can.

How long information is kept

Data on your device stays there until you delete the game or its data. Google keeps the ad data it collects as described in its own policies.

Security

Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit. On your device, the game's files are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles information, for example by adding a new service, we'll update this policy and the effective date above, and describe important changes in the game's release notes.
